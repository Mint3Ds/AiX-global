# Visit2Value synthetic fixtures

Every business, person, review, source ID, employee, and outcome in these files is team-created synthetic material. No public review was copied or scraped. Source names are explicitly simulated and do not indicate platform access.

## Hotel: the complete judge scenario

`hotel/reviews.seed.json` contains 244 historical reviews for fictional Harbour House Hotel, split between Central and Garden properties and three simulated sources. They include English, Malay, mixed wording, specific praise, vague positive and negative opinions, suggestions, complaints, and mixed multi-point reviews. Twenty-four Central source reviews mention room air-conditioning not cooling; 13 mention insect sightings, including some reviews that mention both. Other topics include check-in, room cleanliness, breakfast, noise, staff, amenities, pricing, accessibility, Wi-Fi, and beds. Each observation can point to the original review and exact quote; counts are distinct source reviews, not unique guests.

`hotel/reviews.analysis.json` contains deterministic labelled screening and observation expectations for **all 244** historical hotel reviews. The seed stores these as `fixture`, not as Jev/OpenAI output. `hotel/reviews.demo-new.json` is a separate, unseeded incoming review about warm AC, a small bathroom insect, and helpful front-desk staff. `hotel/reviews.demo-analysis.json` labels its three exact-quote observations. In fixture mode, enter its text exactly and use a fresh synthetic source review ID. It adds one source review to the existing AC and insect issues while preserving the distinct praise point. In live mode, new text uses real Jev/OpenAI provider calls when configured.

The hotel dataset is reproducibly generated by `python3 scripts/generate-hotel-fixtures.py`. The committed JSON files are the runtime source; teammates do not need Python for normal setup. Regeneration is for intentional fixture edits, and ordinary seed runs never overwrite existing database evidence or owner corrections.

## Restaurant and salon

`reviews.seed.json` contains 80 pre-existing Nusa Table restaurant reviews for Sunway and Bangsar. `reviews.demo-new.json` contains five unseeded example inputs, and `reviews.expected-analysis.json` contains the earlier labelled subset. The original restaurant fixtures and workflow remain available as a secondary business.

`hotel/salon.seed.json` adds 18 reviews for fictional Luma Salon. `hotel/salon.analysis.json` labels their observations. The salon has one branch and demonstrates reuse of the review/observation/theme pipeline without attempting a second full judge scenario.

## Fixture semantics and limitations

The fixture lookup requires exact text and business. Generic opinions stay searchable but do not create observations. A changed or unknown review in fixture mode becomes `needs_review`, or can be processed in live mode. Every labelled quote is checked as an exact substring of the source. Hotel themes are useful starting examples, but live extraction and matching can propose new topics beyond the seed catalog. These synthetic examples demonstrate workflow behavior; they do not measure model accuracy or real-world prevalence.

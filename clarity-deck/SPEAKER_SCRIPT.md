# Clarity — slide-by-slide speaker script

**No official time limit was found in the supplied materials**, so this is built as an adjustable timeline. Two variants are marked:

- **Full (~6:30 narration + ~3:00 live demo ≈ 9–10 min).**
- **Tight 5-min rehearsal variant:** say only the **bold** opening line of each slide's narration, skip slides 9 where noted, and cut the demo to the 3 starred ★ steps.

Each slide lists: **⏱ duration · 🎞 animation cue · ⌨ keypress · ➡ transition · 🎯 purpose · 🏅 criterion.**
Press keys on the beats marked ⌨. Nothing auto-advances.

> **Handoff points** are marked 【A】/【B】 if two presenters split the pitch. Suggested split: **A** presents slides 1–6, **B** runs the live demo (slide 7) and closes 8–10. Adjust freely.

## 3 · The problem  【A】
**⏱ 0:45–1:35 · 🎞 source chips drift in; 244 counts up; press to reveal the two stats · ⌨ `→` ×2 · ➡ `→` to Insight**

> **"The problem isn't a lack of feedback — it's what happens after it arrives."**
> "Meet an independent hotel: Harbour House. 244 reviews, three platforms, two properties, and no one clear owner of the follow-up."
> *(press `→`)* "Logging and triaging a single complaint by hand takes ten to fifteen minutes — that's a published benchmark, Johnston 2001."
> *(press `→`)* "And only 31.5% of reviews ever get a response at all — Proserpio and Zervas, 2017. The recurring, fixable problems just sink."

🎯 Quantified, cited pain + a specific SME persona. 🏅 Problem–Solution Fit · Impact & Feasibility.

## 4 · One review. Three truths.  【A】  ★ signature
**⏱ 1:35–2:30 · 🎞 press once to highlight the three spans in the review; press again ×3 to fly out the observation cards · ⌨ `→` ×4 · ➡ `→` to Solution**

> "Here's the core idea. One real review from our synthetic hotel data." *(read it)*
> *(press `→` — highlights appear)* "A sentiment tool gives this **one** score. That's useless to an operator."
> *(press `→` ×3 — cards appear one by one)* "Clarity reads it as three distinct truths: a warm-AC issue, an insect sighting — severity three — and genuine praise for the front desk. Each one keeps the customer's **exact words**. Our `verifyEvidence` check rejects any quote the system can't find verbatim in the original — in English or Malay."

🎯 The differentiator made concrete and memorable. 🏅 Innovation & Creativity · Technical Execution.

## 6 · The system  【A】  ★ signature
**⏱ 3:05–4:05 · 🎞 walk the six nodes; the amber "human control" band draws around steps 4 & 6 · ⌨ `→` ×6 · ➡ `→` to Demo**

> "Six connected steps — words to work." *(press through them)*
> "Intake saves the review verbatim. Extract — Jev screens, OpenAI extracts. Group — matched into issues with distinct counts and a priority reason."
> *(on step 4, band appears)* "Then automation **stops**. The owner approves. Staff report, owner verifies — all append-only in the activity log. And every reply is read by a human before it's posted."
> "The amber band is the point: we're deliberate about where the machine hands control back to the person. The Orbyte handoff here is a **simulated** payload — we're honest that there's no live external API yet."

🎯 Technical depth + human-in-command + honesty. 🏅 Technical Execution · Impact & Feasibility.
